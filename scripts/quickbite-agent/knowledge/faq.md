# QuickBite Customer Support FAQ

## Order Statuses

### Pending
The order has been created and is waiting for restaurant acceptance.

### Accepted
The restaurant has accepted the order and will begin preparing it.

### Preparing
The restaurant is currently preparing the order.

### Out for Delivery
The order has left the restaurant and is being delivered.

### Delivered
The order has been delivered.

### Cancelled
The order has been cancelled.

## Order Information

Customers can check their order status and order details through QuickBite.

## Delivery

A delivery record is created when an order is accepted.

Delivery statuses include:

- Assigned
- Picked Up
- Out for Delivery
- Delivered

## Menu

Customers can view available QuickBite menu items and their prices.

## Support Policy

Do not invent delivery times, preparation times, refund policies, contact numbers,
discounts, guarantees, or other policies that are not explicitly documented here.

If the available information does not answer the customer's question, state that
the information is not available and, when appropriate, retrieve relevant
information from Salesforce.
